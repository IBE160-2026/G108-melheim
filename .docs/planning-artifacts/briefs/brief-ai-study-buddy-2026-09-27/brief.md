# Product Brief: AI Study Buddy

## Executive Summary

Many students find studying boring. When they have a few free minutes, they pick up their phone and scroll or play a game instead of opening their notes.

AI Study Buddy is a website that makes studying feel more like a game. Students upload their own notes, and AI turns them into summaries, flashcards and quizzes. They can practise alone, or challenge friends to quiz battles in the subjects they are studying. Points, streaks and leaderboards give them a reason to come back every day.

AI Study Buddy works in the browser on both PC and mobile, so no download is needed. We start with students at Norwegian universities and colleges, where most students already use AI in their studies.

## The Problem

- **Studying loses to the phone.** Students spend their free time scrolling and gaming. Studying feels slow and boring, so it gets put off until just before the exam.
- **Students often study in a way that doesn't work well.** Many just re-read their notes. Research shows that testing yourself with quizzes and flashcards helps you remember much more, but making them yourself takes a lot of time.
- **Studying alone is hard to stick with.** Nothing reminds you to keep going, and there is no one to compete with or be motivated by.

The result is cramming, stress and weaker exam results.

## The Solution

AI Study Buddy works in three simple steps:

1. **Upload.** The student uploads lecture notes, slides or other study material.
2. **Practise.** AI creates a summary, flashcards and quiz questions from the material. The student can practise alone at their own pace.
3. **Compete.** The student picks a subject they have practised and challenges friends to a quiz. Winning gives points (XP), daily use builds a streak, and leaderboards show who is on top among friends and in each subject.

## What Makes This Different

Other tools already exist. Quizlet and Google NotebookLM can make flashcards and quizzes with AI, Gizmo lets friends quiz each other, and Kahoot is popular for quizzes in the classroom. AI Study Buddy stands out in three ways:

- **It competes with the phone, not with other study tools.** Most study tools add a small game on top. We start with the fun of competing with friends and connect it to the student's own course material.
- **Made for university students, by students, with no teacher needed.** Kahoot and similar tools are mostly used by teachers in schools. With AI Study Buddy, students challenge each other directly.
- **Made for Norway first.** Most big competitors focus on English-speaking users. We support both Norwegian and English material from the start.

## Who This Serves

**University and college students**, starting in Norway. They use their phones all the time, like games, and would rather do anything than read notes. They want to pass their exams with less stress. For them, success means using spare minutes to actually learn, feeling more prepared for exams, and having fun doing it with friends.

- Users say they feel better prepared for their exams.
- About 5% of active users pay for Premium.

## Scope (First Version)

**Included:**
- Creating a user account and adding friends
- Uploading notes (text and PDF)
- AI-made summaries, flashcards and quizzes
- Practising alone
- Quiz battles against friends, sorted by subject
- Points, streaks and leaderboards
- A website that works well on both PC and mobile

**Not included (for now):**
- Apps in the App Store or Google Play
- Tools for teachers
- Connection to university systems such as Canvas
- Playing against strangers

## Risks

- **The AI can make mistakes.** Wrong questions are bad for learning and make competitions unfair. Users should be able to see where in their notes an answer comes from and report errors.
- **Big competitors could copy us.** Gizmo and Kahoot (a Norwegian company) are much bigger. We need to move fast and build a loyal group of students.
- **AI costs money.** The more students use it, the more it costs. Limits on the free version help keep costs under control.
- **Privacy.** Student data must be handled according to GDPR, and uploaded material may be protected by copyright.

## Vision

In 2–3 years, AI Study Buddy is the place where students at Nordic universities go to revise. Every subject has active players, friends push each other before exams, and practising with a quiz is as natural as opening a game. After that, the goal is to grow to universities across Europe.
